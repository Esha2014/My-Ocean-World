Fun Ocean Facts 🌊

Learn fascinating facts about the ocean and sea animals.

Amazing Ocean Facts

🌍

Most of Earth

Oceans cover more than 70% of Earth's surface and contain most of the planet's water.

🌊

Deep Waters

The deepest known part of the ocean is the Mariana Trench, which is deeper than Mount Everest is tall.

💨

Ocean Oxygen

Tiny ocean plants called phytoplankton produce a large amount of the oxygen we breathe.

Fun Sea Animal Facts

🦈

Sharks

Sharks have existed for more than 400 million years, long before the dinosaurs appeared.

🐢

Sea Turtles

Some sea turtles can travel thousands of kilometres and return to the beach where they were born.

🐙

Octopuses

Octopuses have three hearts, blue blood, and eight arms covered with suction cups.

Did You Know? 🐋

The blue whale is the largest animal ever known to have lived on Earth. Its heart can be as large as a small car!

More Interesting Facts

🐬 Dolphins

Dolphins communicate using clicks, whistles, and body movements. They can also recognise themselves in mirrors.

🪼 Jellyfish

Jellyfish have no bones, brain, or heart. They use their tentacles to catch food and protect themselves.

⭐ Starfish

Starfish are not actually fish. They are animals that can regrow some of their lost arms.

🪸 Coral Reefs

Coral reefs are home to about 25% of all marine species, even though they cover less than 1% of the ocean floor.
