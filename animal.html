All About Sharks 🦈

Sharks are ancient fish that have lived in Earth's oceans for more than 400 million years.

They help keep marine ecosystems healthy by controlling populations of other sea animals.

🦷 Powerful Teeth

Sharks have several rows of teeth. Lost teeth are replaced by new teeth from behind.

🌊 Great Senses

Sharks can detect movement in the water and sense electrical signals from other animals.

⏳ Ancient Animals

Sharks appeared millions of years before dinosaurs and have survived many changes on Earth.

All About Sea Turtles 🐢

Sea turtles are reptiles that spend most of their lives swimming through warm and cool ocean waters.

They travel long distances and return to beaches to lay their eggs.

🐚 Strong Shells

A sea turtle's shell protects its body from danger and helps it survive in the ocean.

🌍 Long Journeys

Sea turtles can migrate thousands of kilometres between feeding and nesting areas.

🌿 Ocean Helpers

Green sea turtles help keep seagrass habitats healthy by feeding on seagrass.

All About Octopuses 🐙

Octopuses are intelligent sea animals with eight arms covered in powerful suction cups.

They can squeeze through tiny spaces and use camouflage to hide from predators.

🎨 Colour Changing

Octopuses can change their colour and skin texture to blend into their surroundings.

🧠 Very Intelligent

Octopuses can solve puzzles, remember solutions, and learn from their surroundings.

💨 Ink Defence

When threatened, an octopus can release a cloud of ink to confuse predators and escape.
