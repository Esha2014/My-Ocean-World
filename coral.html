What Are Coral Reefs?

Coral reefs are underwater ecosystems built by tiny animals called coral polyps. Over time, these polyps create hard skeletons that form large reef structures.

Reefs provide shelter, food, and breeding areas for thousands of fish and other marine animals.

Why Coral Reefs Matter

🐠 Homes for Animals

Coral reefs provide a safe habitat for fish, turtles, crabs, sea stars, and many other ocean animals.

🌊 Protect Coastlines

Reefs help reduce the power of waves and protect coastal communities from storms and erosion.

🌍 Support People

Healthy reefs support fishing, tourism, food supplies, and jobs for communities around the world.

Did You Know? 🪸

Coral reefs cover less than one percent of the ocean floor, but they support about 25 percent of all marine species.

How Can We Protect Coral Reefs?

♻️ Reduce Pollution

Use less plastic, recycle properly, and keep rubbish away from beaches and waterways.

🌱 Make Sustainable Choices

Choose sustainable seafood and support organisations that protect marine habitats.
